SPECIFICATIONS FINALIZED

We're building BlackMarket AI, a local Python-based Binance market intelligence and self-learning paper-trading system.

Its purpose is to continuously observe the entire eligible Binance Spot and USDⓈ-M Futures market, identify opportunities, test its own predictions, learn from outcomes, and send structured trade proposals to Telegram.

The system will not need

- Binance API keys.
- Binance account access.
- OpenAI or other cloud AI APIs.
- Paid AI subscriptions.
- Cloud-hosted machine-learning services.
- Real-money trading permissions.

It will need an internet connection for public Binance market data and a Telegram bot token for notifications.

### Market discovery rules

The discovery engine will

1. Retrieve exchange information on startup.
2. Select actively trading Spot symbols.
3. Select actively trading USDⓈ-M contracts.
4. Record symbol precision, tick size, lot size and minimum notional.
5. Detect newly listed and delisted instruments.
6. Refresh the market universe periodically.

We will monitor all eligible symbols, including non-USDT Spot pairs, but the initial paper-trading engine will prioritize USDT-quoted instruments. Other quote currencies can be supported through locally calculated conversion prices.

### Efficient scanning strategy

Instead of subscribing to nine candle streams for every symbol, use a tiered approach.

Tier 1 — Entire market

Continuous

Broad ticker streams, price changes, volume and market-wide statistics.

Tier 2 — Potential opportunities

Frequent

Analyze indicators and candle data on all nine timeframes for symbols showing meaningful activity.

Tier 3 — High-priority opportunities

Deep analysis

Order book, funding, open interest, multi-timeframe confirmation and ML prediction.

This is how we get broad market coverage without overwhelming a modest computer.

WebSocket connections must be rotated and reconnected safely. Binance documents stream-count and message-rate limits, so the connection manager will enforce limits rather than blindly subscribing to everything.&#x20;

## 4. Market intelligence and strategy system

The strategy engine will have five initial strategy families.

 Strategy         Primary purpose                       
 ---------------  ------------------------------------- 
 Momentum         Identify accelerating price movement  
 Trend following  Identify sustained directional trends 
 Breakout         Detect price escaping consolidation   
 Mean reversion   Identify unusually extended prices    
 Reversal         Detect potential trend exhaustion     

Each strategy returns a standardized result

```
{    symbol BTCUSDT,    market FUTURES,    strategy momentum,    direction LONG,    timeframe 15m,    entry 87250.0,    stop_loss 86700.0,    take_profit [88100.0, 89000.0],    raw_score 82,    reasons [        Bullish momentum,        Volume expansion,        Higher timeframe confirmation    ]}
```

These values are an example schema, not a real signal.

### Multi-timeframe intelligence

The nine timeframes have different responsibilities.

 Timeframe  Role                            
 ---------  ------------------------------- 
 1m         Entry timing and micro momentum 
 3m         Short-term confirmation         
 5m         Intraday momentum               
 15m        Primary setup detection         
 30m        Intraday trend confirmation     
 1h         Directional bias                
 4h         Major trend                     
 1d         Daily market structure          
 1w         Long-term market regime         

The decision engine should not require all nine timeframes to agree. Instead, it should assign different weights to each timeframe and recognize conflicting conditions.

For example, a bullish 5m breakout against a bearish 4h trend may receive a lower score or be classified as a countertrend setup.

### Signal decision rules

BUY  SELL

SPOT

- BUY favorable bullish setup.
- SELL exit or reduce an existing simulated Spot position.
- WAIT no validated opportunity.

  LONG  SHORT

  FUTURES
- LONG favorable bullish futures setup.
- SHORT favorable bearish futures setup.
- WAIT no validated opportunity.

The engine should distinguish between a signal to enter, a signal to exit, and a signal to hold. A SELL signal should not automatically mean opening a short position.

## 5. Hybrid self-learning architecture

This is where BlackMarket AI becomes more than an ordinary trading scanner.

I recommend three connected learning systems.

### A. Adaptive strategy selector

This system evaluates strategy performance under different market regimes.

For example

- Breakout strategies in trending markets.
- Mean-reversion strategies in sideways markets.
- Momentum strategies in high-volume markets.
- Reversal strategies near exhaustion conditions.

It updates strategy weights using rolling performance and risk-adjusted results.

### B. Local machine-learning models

The initial models will be

 Model                   Responsibility                  
 ----------------------  ------------------------------- 
 Random Forest           Initial predictive baseline     
 XGBoost                 Nonlinear signal classification 
 River model             Optional incremental learning   
 Probability calibrator  Improve probability reliability 

The models will predict outcomes such as

- Upward movement.
- Downward movement.
- No meaningful movement.

They should predict probabilities for a defined future horizon, not pretend to know the exact future price.

Each prediction should include a forecast horizon, model version, training cutoff, and calibration status.

### C. Learning feedback loop

1. Collect historical market features.
2. Generate a strategy proposal.
3. Record the prediction and its reasoning.
4. Wait for the defined outcome horizon.
5. Calculate the actual outcome after simulated costs.
6. Compare prediction with actual performance.
7. Update strategy weights and training datasets.
8. Validate the new model before promotion.

Important learning safeguards

- No training on unfinished future information.
- No random traintest splitting of time-series data.
- Use walk-forward validation.
- Maintain an untouched out-of-sample evaluation period.
- Keep previous model versions.
- Roll back a model if its performance deteriorates.
- Don't treat every paper-trade win as proof of predictive accuracy.

The system can learn autonomously, but it must not be allowed to rewrite its own source code or risk controls.

  Suggested initial simulation safeguards

   Risk per trade  0.5% 
   Maximum daily loss  2% 
   Maximum aggregate open risk  3% 
   Futures leverage ceiling  3x 
   Maximum concurrent positions  5 per market 

These are conservative starting parameters for simulation, not proven optimal settings. They should be configurable and tested.

For example, 0.5% risk on a 500 USDT account equals 2.50 USDT of planned loss at the stop, before gaps and execution differences.

The simulator must calculate risk based on stop distance and position size, not simply multiply account balance by leverage.

### Trade lifecycle

Every paper trade will follow this process

`PROPOSED → VALIDATED → OPEN → PARTIALLY FILLED → CLOSED`

It can also transition to

`REJECTED`, `EXPIRED`, or `CANCELLED`.

Every transition must be persisted. This is important for recovery if the PC shuts down unexpectedly.

## 7. Intelligence communication system

You specifically said you want the bot to communicate with its own intelligence.

I recommend implementing an internal communication protocol called the BlackMarket Intelligence Bus.

It will allow individual components to exchange structured messages without depending on one another directly.

For example

```
[MARKET SCANNER]
    
     BTCUSDT momentum detected
    v
[FEATURE ENGINE]
    
     Bullish momentum + volume expansion
    v
[STRATEGY ENGINE]
    
     Breakout strategy activated
    v
[ML PREDICTOR]
    
     Estimated outcome probabilities
    v
[DECISION ENGINE]
    
     Validate risk and signal quality
    v
[PAPER TRADING ENGINE]
    
     Simulated execution and outcome
    v
[LEARNING ENGINE]
    
     Evaluate and update strategy performance
    v
[INTELLIGENCE MEMORY]
```

### Internal message format

Every internal message should follow a common structure

```
{    event_id unique-id,    event_type SIGNAL_PROPOSED,    timestamp 2026-11-16T163000Z,    source decision_engine,    symbol BTCUSDT,    market FUTURES,    payload {        direction LONG,        confidence 0.78,        strategy breakout    }}
```

The event bus will support

- Market data events.
- Strategy events.
- Prediction events.
- Trade proposal events.
- Paper execution events.
- Learning events.
- Risk alerts.
- System health events.

Use `asyncio.Queue` for the initial implementation. Add persistent event logging in SQLite so that critical events can be replayed after a restart.

The intelligence engine will also have access to its own historical performance database. It can query questions such as

- Which strategy has performed best in this market regime
- Which timeframes have generated the most reliable signals
- How often did a particular setup reach its first target
- Which conditions produce the most false breakouts
- How has the model performed over its last 100 predictions

This is structured quantitative self-analysis, not an LLM pretending to reason.

## 15. Final security and reliability rules

These should be non-negotiable

- No Binance order endpoints in the application.
- No Binance API key authentication code.
- No automatic real-money execution.
- No model modification of risk limits.
- No trading on stale or incomplete market data.
- No promotion of unvalidated models.
- No deleting losing trades from learning history.
- No fabricated fills or historical performance.
- No exposure of Telegram credentials.
- Automatic database backups and model checkpoints.

The paper simulator should be completely isolated from any future live-execution project.
